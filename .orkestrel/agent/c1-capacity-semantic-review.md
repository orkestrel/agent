## Required protocol distinction

Capacity preflight can legitimately ask whether the instrument executes and accounts for the entire prescribed workload under finite bounds, separately from whether the model finishes every task. Requiring every product to succeed makes that preflight also a model-competence gate. That stricter gate is coherent, but it prevents the intended comparison from measuring the model weakness now observed. Revising it must be explicit, prospective, and equally applied.

The candidate phrase “complete observation rows” must mean a present, valid observation record for every attempted goal. It cannot mean `observation.complete === true`. In `Trial.#observeWork`, that field means a nonpartial, nonempty product without error/overflow. Ordinary step exhaustion correctly has `observation.complete: false`; preserve that value and its unsuccessful row. Rewriting it to true or continuing to require true would respectively hide the weakness or leave the original conflation intact.

Likewise `failure: continue` does not turn all child exits into zero. `Trial.execute` returns 3 after a fully attempted horizon when `unserved > 0`, even though run status becomes `complete`. The successor must inspect retained evidence before distinguishing that documented semantic exit 3 from operational exit 3. A blanket acceptance of code 3 is invalid.

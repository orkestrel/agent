# Why g06 almost never passes (2026-10-09)

Goal `g06-kenji-shipping` asks: "Kenji Nakamura is asking whether his replacement kettle has shipped yet. Check the order and write the reply I can send him." It expects the tracking number PW-6013-2280 and forbids the 2026-10-12 delivery date in several forms, plus any calendar date at all through one pattern. The required lookup of LH-81660 returns "Shipped 2026-10-07 by Parcelway, tracking PW-6013-2280, carrier estimated delivery 2026-10-12". The rule is seed message 6, the second sentence of an escalation rule: "Third rule: copy Priya Raman from the refunds team on every escalation. And never promise a customer a delivery date in writing." The assistant's acknowledgment, seed message 7, widens it: "no delivery dates go in customer replies."

## Census

Over every recorded benchmark reply (`/tmp/claude-0/.../scratchpad/g06-rows.json`, probes, calibration rows, and empty replies left out), g06 passed 5 of 99:

| Run | Model and thinking | Arm | Reply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `v9/a1-control-v7` | 2B, off | full view | correct |
| `v9/a4-refined-v5` | 2B, off | refined briefing | correct, byte-identical to the next row |
| `v9/a5-records-v5` | 2B, off | records | correct |
| `v9/a5-records-v6` | 2B, off | records | right facts, but a note to the shift lead, not a reply to send |
| `v9/a3-refined-v1` | 2B, off | refined briefing | false pass: "your replacement kettle arrived today!", which the lookup contradicts |

Every `v10` row fails: 0 of 38 for the 2B with thinking and the 4B with and without thinking, in every arm. Of the 94 failures, 88 write the 2026-10-12 estimate into the reply, and 81 of those are otherwise right, tracking number included.

## Cause

- **Not the harness.** In all 5 passes and the failures beside them, the model called `lookup_order`, held the lookup result with the estimate as its last message, and had the rule in its prompt. No prompt was truncated, markdown is stripped before scoring, and thinking never reaches the scorer.
- **Not the scorer, in the measured data.** Removing the any-date pattern changes 0 of the 94 verdicts; hand-written correct replies with no date pass.
- **The model relays the estimate.** Within an arm, the passing and failing requests differ only in the request sentence of each reworded copy, and no wording passes in every arm, so the 5 passes are what a reworded request does at temperature 0, not a condition that works.
- **The thinking traces confirm it.** Of 19 g06 answer calls with thinking (`t2`, `t2w`, `t2a`, `t4`), 17 never cite the rule and write the date. The 2 that cite it (4B, `t4-control-v2`, `t4-records-v2`) write the date anyway: "I'm just reporting what's already in the system (the estimated delivery), not promising anything new."

## Defects in the goal

- **The rule says "promise".** Seed 6 forbids promising a delivery date; it does not plainly forbid relaying a carrier's estimate, and the 4B read it that way. The stricter reading lives in the acknowledgment (seed 7) and in the scenario's notes, which the model never sees.
- **The arms see different rule texts.** Records and the briefing arms never carry assistant text, so 45 of 76 g06 answer requests show only "never promise …", while the full view also shows "no delivery dates go in customer replies".
- **The pattern is broader than the rule.** It fails a correct reply that gives the 2026-10-07 ship date or a dated header; it has decided no verdict so far.
- **The scorer is too lenient elsewhere.** It passes a made-up "arrived today" reply and a reply with no customer draft.
- **It cannot discriminate.** Only g06 forbids a value that its own required lookup returns in answer to the question it asks. Every arm and model sits at the floor, so g06 caps every run at 9 of 10 and says nothing about records against the full view.

## Ruling

g06 measures one model behavior, relaying salient lookup data that a distant, softly worded rule forbids, and both arms fail it equally. Read no arm comparison from it. To make it a fair test, the scorer unit U2 states the rule plainly in every arm (reword seed 6 to "never put a delivery date, a carrier estimate included, in a customer reply"), scores only the customer-facing draft, fails a reply with no draft or with a claimed delivery, and limits the date pattern to a date in a sentence about arrival, delivery, or an estimate.

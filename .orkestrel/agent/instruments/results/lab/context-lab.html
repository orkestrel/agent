Larkspur Context Lab

A 2B model works one support shift: 10 requests in a row, each needing a fact from 48 earlier messages, inside a 3,072-token window. Each strategy decides what the model reads per request.

Where things stand

    Scores

    Round A, one run each, graded out of 60 (correct, complete, faithful) on the fixed harness, where every request gets a reply. The tick marks the control. Differences under about 4 points are noise.

    Passes across reworded copies

    Each copy rewords the 10 requests and keeps every fact, so the spread across copies is the noise. Scored by the harness’s checks, pass or fail per request. A strategy shows a gain only when its mean difference from the full view, minus twice its standard error, stays above 0.

    Per request: how many copies passed. Requests every strategy fails point at the model; requests one strategy fails point at its memory.

    Prompt size per request

    Round A. The control grows about 300 tokens per request and passes the 3,072-token window at request 3; the briefing stays under 2,900.

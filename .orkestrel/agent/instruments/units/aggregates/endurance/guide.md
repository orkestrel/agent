Request repetition is counted explicitly. The readings are not provider usage, calibrated request prices, selected context, or generated history.

The declared bounds measure maximum request text, one shift's arrivals plus four requests, one goal's unique atom-linked sources, and the unique source union supporting active fact records at each goal. The last includes historical receipts retained for reconciliation, and measures their whole source paragraphs rather than compressed fact values. It is a conservative static corpus bound, not a claim that every record is selected or that the model retains all of it. Development must meet or exceed evaluation on these per-goal and current-source bounds; both initial seeds and individual shifts remain below 3,072 estimated tokens. Checkpoint populations occur every fourth shift, including the final interval.

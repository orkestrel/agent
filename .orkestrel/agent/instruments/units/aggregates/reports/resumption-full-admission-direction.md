# Prove service controls before full-view qualification

Root accepts a bounded deployed-service proof as the next full-view unit. No capacity implementation or trajectory is qualified by this ruling. The requested window was clamped during the retained Qwen run; full-view evidence must distinguish the derived history bound, requested window, and actual serving capacity. See [the observed clamp](../diagnostics/q2-full-capacity/README.md).

The objective analyst's clarification replaces proposed per-request process/log attestation with explicit removal controls and an immediate loaded-model snapshot under exclusive serial use. Root accepts that narrower direction conditionally. See [the immutable clarification](resumption-full-admission-clarification.md). The original proposal remains retained and is superseded only where this ruling and the clarification say so.

Official Ollama 0.40.2 source supports explicit input-truncation and context-shift controls. The adapter's disabled-shift branch omits the enable flag and inherits its environment; the runner also accepts a named environment override. Root directly checked those paths. The installed executable has not been matched byte-for-byte to the source, so deployment controls remain necessary. See [the Ollama adapter](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ollama/ollama/v0.40.2/llm/llama_server.go), [the scheduler](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ollama/ollama/v0.40.2/server/sched.go), and [the runner option](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/631109b34/common/arg.cpp).

The deployed proof must retain successful requests, input-overflow rejection paired with adequate-context reachability, and a reached generation-capacity boundary. Preserve model/backend identities, raw wire, immediate loaded-context snapshots, and bounded runner observations that relate the snapshot to per-sequence capacity. A natural early stop or parser error leaves the relevant control inconclusive. Use diagnostic data outside calibration and evaluation populations and predeclare request, byte, output-token, and wall limits. Cover the serving paths actually used by the requested models.

Bind the daemon's nonsecret context-shift setting and serial allocation. An interactive-shell environment reading cannot establish the already running daemon's inherited state. If the named setting cannot be observed, finish the active focused test and launch a controlled successor daemon with explicit settings and retained logs. This operational revision belongs in the evidence; it must not be described as unchanged wire or deployment. No broad environment dump or secret inspection is authorized.

Only after that proof can a successor instrument add explicit controls to every decision call, retain complete chronological request history, and take a model-matched context snapshot before another service request. Initial calls, tool continuations, and recovery share that contract across the comparison arms. A model error, missing usage, ambiguous capacity, or length finish prevents trajectory qualification. Judge and summarizer settings remain separate recorded role settings; do not change them to avoid reloads.

The canonical revision must freeze a qualification ceiling before its trajectory. Loaded configured capacity must cover that ceiling; actual prompt plus completion must stay strictly below it. An oversized conservative history bound alone does not disqualify a finite observed trajectory. Fresh development qualification follows the successor build. No answer-quality score enters these resource decisions.

Root will not add a tokenizer, capacity-discovery library, general process-attestation framework, or per-request log parser for this contract. The existing fetch and artifact boundaries own the bounded implementation after the deployed controls establish its assumptions.

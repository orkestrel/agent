## Grading conventions

Semantic judgments assess the complete meaning of the answer, including material contradictions and invented actions or policy. Correct expected words do not rescue an incorrect current state. Equivalent dates and amounts, harmless wording differences, and optional omissions do not cause failure. Earlier values named only to reject or correct them remain acceptable.

The controlling protocol is `.orkestrel/agent/instruments/harness/bench/variants/long/v1.json#/ledger/system`. A recall containing an old lookup result does not constitute a fresh lookup.

The `systemTool` field applies the fresh-lookup instruction to requests concerning conversation orders or accounts, including a named customer whose identifiers are established in the eligible conversation. The `listedTool` field separately applies each authored goal's explicit list with those protocol substitutions. A list containing only `send_reply` does not waive the system rule.

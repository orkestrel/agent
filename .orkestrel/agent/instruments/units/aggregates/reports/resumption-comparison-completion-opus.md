- **Support-safety qualification.** Only a strict separating fit qualifies (`.orkestrel/agent/instruments/harness/bench5/refit/helpers.ts:258-299`). Exploratory admission never writes `separated` or `accepted`, and strict refusals stay primary evidence.
- **Adoption recommendation.** This needs a qualified matrix plus a qualified sustained pair that meets `.orkestrel/agent/instruments/units/aggregates/endurance-plan.md:45`. Exploratory evidence can be reported to the user. The experiment issues no recommendation from it. Adopting on exploratory evidence would be the user's own decision, outside the experiment's verdict.

## Proposal: challenges to root's candidate

I accept most of root's direction. The challenges:

1. **The candidate covers only the matrix.** It leaves the mandatory sustained comparison unreachable (findings 4–6). U2 is required.
2. **Derive `agree`; don't declare it.**
   - Store the cutoff in the policy, and have `readDevelopment` recompute it from the bound diagnosis readings with `selectMajorityCutoff`. This picks the highest grid value whose `measureRecall` admission is ≥ 0.5 for both occurrences and distinct identities.
   - Place the helper in `.orkestrel/agent/instruments/harness/bench5/matrix/helpers.ts`, not in the refit helpers. That file is already on the historical-source allowlist (`.orkestrel/agent/instruments/harness/bench5/matrix/helpers.ts:466-477`). Editing refit source would break the q2 bridge's byte checks.
   - Extract the identity deduplication from `fitReadings` as an export, without changing the results `fitReadings` returns.
3. **Keep the l13 policy readable.** Make `calibration` optional; when it is absent, the predecessor is the calibration freeze. The l13 policy must still validate under the amended reader.
4. **Don't regenerate q2 calibration on the final source.** The inputs are identical (`.orkestrel/agent/instruments/units/aggregates/reports/resumption-matrix-calibration-custody.md:15`). A rerun would only retry a failed qualification, which is selection by repetition. The l9 refusal stands for q2 in both thinking modes.

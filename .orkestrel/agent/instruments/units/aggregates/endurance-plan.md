## Bound the workload

Author a held-out support-operations trajectory with 96 work goals over 24 shifts. Each shift contains a queue decision sheet, a resolution packet, a reconciliation, and a handoff. The packet includes a calculated remedy, a customer draft, and an internal authorization note. Later goals refer to the arm's actual earlier products. A draft never proves that an external action occurred. External confirmations arrive on a fixed schedule shared by both arms.

Use a bounded queue of 12 distinct customer/order threads. Carry unfinished obligations, deadlines, blockers, approval requirements, corrections, withdrawals, superseded contacts, versioned lookups, closed and reopened cases, and delayed receipts. Include early and late opportunities for each transition category. Keep an early obligation open until the final interval and return to a dormant case after at least 8 shifts without mentioning it. Give each paragraph a scored decision, downstream dependency, competing obligation, or explicit transition. Add no padding or copied transcript repetition to reach a size target.

Use the fixed work horizon rather than expanding prose to meet a token quota. ## Score sustained work

Freeze atomic requirements for active facts and calculations, usable deliverables, outstanding obligations, corrections and withdrawals, and evidence or recovery. A fully acceptable product satisfies every mandatory atom and has no critical violation. Critical violations include fabricated completion, action under withdrawn authorization, action using a superseded destination, or closure without required evidence. Historical mention of an ended value is permitted only when its inactive status is explicit.

Score facts against the active external truth and continuity against the arm's actual prior work. Repetition of an earlier wrong answer earns no factual credit. A specific blocker and an actionable next step can satisfy a blocked-work requirement; generic hedging cannot replace feasible work. Verify required lookups from actual receipts and active tool versions.

Blind the audit to arm, model, selection, and summary metadata. Include the current request, actual work product, relevant earlier same-arm products, available external evidence, tool receipts, and private rubric. Randomize opaque item identities with a recorded seed and retain the key separately. Plant prefrozen stale-action, false-completion, and ambiguous-value controls; a missed control invalidates the affected audit batch. Preserve ambiguity bounds and identity leaks. Keep lexical scores diagnostic and separate.

Report paired wins, losses, ties, critical violations, fully acceptable products, obligation outcomes, and dimension rates at each checkpoint. Treat the trajectory as a dependent experimental unit. Do not infer independent-trial significance from its goals or call rewordings independent worlds. Report actual exposure, supported maintenance chains, absent transitions, recovery, and resource invalidity separately from answer quality.

## Bound execution and decide

Materialize finite call, token, retry, and wall limits before evaluation from the fixed implementation and development readings. Include decision, filing, summarizer, support, and flush costs. Freeze arm order from the manifest seed. Run arms serially on the same host and disclose order-sensitive timing. Charge development and calibration separately from evaluation.

Stop at the frozen horizon or a declared integrity/resource failure. Preserve the last complete common checkpoint and invalid suffix. Missing usage, truncation, length finishes, and capacity overflow invalidate affected evidence; they are not ordinary wrong answers. Exposure collapse is a treatment limitation, not evidence that records have higher answer quality.

A conditional quality recommendation for aggregates requires a valid continuous pair, nonempty treatment on more than half of goals in every checkpoint interval, no aggregate-only critical regression, and a positive lower bound of fully acceptable-product gain both overall and in the final interval. The gain must include completed or repaired downstream work. Report total and incremental cost without inventing a user utility ceiling. Otherwise preserve negative or inconclusive evidence for the final user decision alongside the matrix.

## Reconcile the blind opinions

Root accepts continuous actual histories, a separate held-out world, item-level blind auditing, strict scenario-specific cache custody, and feasibility-only configuration selection from both opinions. Root selects the longer single trajectory to test the user's sustained-work requirement, and the larger feasible configurations first to reduce an uninformative quality floor. Root rejects multiplying the final comparison by paraphrases, increasing endurance resources after matrix preflight, and equating invalid treatment with a quality win for records.

Root selects fresh development calibration with explicit custody. The existing refit binds exact scenario and implementation files, so an unchanged matrix fit cannot silently become an endurance fit. A separate transfer-only fitting phase adds no required capability to this experiment. The maintained-state limitations and fixed admission policy remain in force. Source authoring, runtime integration, independent falsification, and verification must finish before the live freeze.
